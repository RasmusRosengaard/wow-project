Watchlist

Track specific items across every EU realm, no sell realm required.

Instead of "cheap relative to my sell realm," Watchlist asks a different question: is this specific item cheap anywhere in the region, right now? Paste in a TSM group export of the items you care about, and get notified onn Discord when one of the items shows up priced well below its usual EU price on any realm, or a price you set.

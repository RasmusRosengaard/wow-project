Questions

Why is the free tier locked to one sell realm?

Every query re-runs a real analysis against Blizzard's own auction data — it isn't free for us to compute. Capping free accounts to one realm keeps that cost bounded without capping the accuracy of what you see; a subscription removes the realm limit instead of the accuracy.

Is the free tier's data somehow worse or delayed?

No. Every tier reads the exact same validated, inferred-sold-price data, refreshed on the same schedule. The only differences are how many results come back per query and how many sell realms you can pick from.

Can I change my locked realm later?

Not on the free tier — the lock is set the first time you query a realm and stays fixed. Subscribing removes the lock immediately, letting you query and switch between any collected realm.

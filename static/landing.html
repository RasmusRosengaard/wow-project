Realm Arbitrage

You don't need to know which items are worth sniping.

Switch on the default sniper list and it stands over every item on every EU realm, sending anything priced far under its TSM sale average to your Discord. The snipe board is the other half: it prices listings against your own realm.

Watchlist & Discord

Paste a Discord webhook and get notified whenever an item meets your criteria.

  • A default sniper that evaluates items against TSM's EU sale average based on your % preferences. No prior knowledge needed.
  • Manually add items to your watchlist if desired.
  • Set the threshold in raw gold, or as a percentage of the sale average.

Snipe board

Pick the realm you sell on. The board ranks auctions on every other EU realm by how far under your own realm's cheapest price they are.

  • Buy realm, item, buy price, sell price, EU median, sale rate (TSM), sale average (TSM), discount %, and whether you have a character on that realm.
  • Filter by unique transmog, item class, rarity, price and discount.
  • Hide flagged hides obvious bait items and falsely priced items.
  • Not real-time. Blizzard republishes auction data about once an hour, so a snipe can be gone before you arrive.

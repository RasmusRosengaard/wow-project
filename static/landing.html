How it works

Three steps, no manual comparisons across realm forums or Discord price checks.

1

Pick your sell realm

The high-population realm where you actually sell gear. We collect its live auction data hourly.

2

We scan every other EU realm

Every connected realm's current listings, swept on the same schedule — no history needed on the buy side.

3

Only genuine snipes surface

Priced below your own realm's current cheapest listing, net of the 5% AH cut. Nothing else shows up.

Not another sniper

Existing tools compare one listing to another listing. That's not the price that matters.

TSM / Auctionator

Flag an item as cheap because it's priced below other listings of the same item — wherever those happen to be. That comparison can be fiction: cheap next to what, exactly?

Realm Arbitrage

Flags an item only when it's priced below what you'd actually get selling it on your own realm, right now. The number you're compared against is real, because it's the same number you'd charge.

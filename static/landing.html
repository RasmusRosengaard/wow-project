Cross-realm AH validation for World of Warcraft

Somewhere in the EU, this is cheaper.

Realm Arbitrage calculates the real price difference, the arbitrage, between your own realm's current price and every other EU realm's listings for the same item. Not every gap is a genuine bargain: some listings are troll prices or camped jokes from other players, not real value. If it looks worth it, buy it there, move it home through the warband bank, sell it here.
